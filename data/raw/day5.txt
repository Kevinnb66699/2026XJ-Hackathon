一、拓展问答
     请结合文章内容和你的理解回答下列问题。


1. The author argues that social media bans aimed at protecting teenagers may
actually be counterproductive. Do you agree that such bans do more harm than good?
What might be some better ways to keep young people safe online besides complete
bans?
(Hint: Think about the problems of enforcing bans, obscure websites, and online
predators mentioned in the passage.)


2. The article says social media benefits isolated children and helps them feel less
lonely. Why is providing support for lonely or disadvantaged young people
considered an important value that supporters of bans often ignore?
(Hint: Consider teenagers who feel different because of their location, identity, or way
of thinking.)


3. The writer states that social media has become one of the main ways young people
get information about current affairs. Do you believe this change is positive or
negative overall? Should schools and parents help teenagers tell true information from
misinformation instead of banning social media?
(Hint: Compare today’s online information with the past, when youngsters got news
from newspapers and TV bulletins.)
