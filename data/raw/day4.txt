一、视译训练
     请边读下列的英文内容，边直接口头说出中文意思。尽量保持语流连贯，不
回读。


1. People don’t agree on much these days. But one thing brings them together,
whatever their politics: the idea that, because social media harm children and
teenagers, they should be banned from using them.


2. Australia has not banned young teenagers from messaging apps such as WhatsApp,
or from multiplayer online games, because that would have seemed too draconian;
scourges such as cyberbullying will doubtless continue on these. Kids barred from
mainstream sites could flock to obscure ones, and fall victim to predators there.


二、关键词复述
     不要参考原文，看着下面 4 组关键词链条，用英语把文章的主要内容讲出来
（可以想象自己正在演讲论证）。


Part 1: Introduction
Keywords: social media, harm, teenagers, banned, Australia, other countries, support
(Tip: Start by introducing the common belief and what countries are doing.)


Part 2: Reasons for Bans
Keywords: parents, safety, tragedies, waste time, suffer, only members, politicians,
please all votes
(Tip: Explain why people support bans and what harm they fear.)
